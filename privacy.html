Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

HMN Atelier V5 is an independently developed, locally operated AI creative workflow project. This policy describes the Google Drive integration currently used with the desktop application.

Information accessed

When the account holder authorizes the integration, the application requests the Google Drive drive.file permission. This allows access to specific Drive files created or used with the application, rather than general access to all files in the account.

How information is used

The application uses that authorization to upload, archive, list, verify, download and, when explicitly initiated, manage files associated with HMN Atelier V5. This includes selected project media and archives. The public website does not collect Google Drive files or provide a public file browser.

Storage and sharing

Selected files are stored in the authorized Google Drive account and may also remain on the account holder's local computer. Google processes Drive content as a service provider under its own terms and privacy practices. HMN Atelier V5 does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. Files are not intentionally published through this website.

Access control and retention

The account holder controls which files are copied or removed. Archived files remain in Google Drive until deleted by the account holder or by a deliberately authorized operation. Local copies may be retained for active work or recovery. The integration may store an OAuth authorization token locally so that rclone can access the authorized account.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke access in your Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revocation prevents new authorized Drive requests but does not automatically remove files already stored in Google Drive or local files. Those can be deleted separately using Google Drive and local file management.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests about this project, contact l.hellemun@hotmail.com.

Changes

This policy will be updated when the integration's actual data handling or availability changes. Any broader access or external services will require a revised policy and appropriate authorization.
